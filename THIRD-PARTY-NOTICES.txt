not part of this project's MIT license.
  BepInEx: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx  (LGPL-2.1, license text and source code are in that repository)
  It bundles HarmonyX (MIT, https://github.com/BepInEx/HarmonyX) and other components under their own licenses.

The plugin is built against assemblies that BepInEx generates from the player's own copy of the game. No game files are distributed.
